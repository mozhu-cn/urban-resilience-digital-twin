Highlights

- A physics-guided digital twin framework is proposed for dynamic urban flood resilience assessment.
- A terrain-informed cellular flood model captures nonlinear hazard evolution under extreme rainfall.
- Time-delayed cascading failures are modeled through backup-energy depletion in interdependent networks.
- Adaptive restoration optimization improves recovery decisions under flood-constrained transportation.
- The framework is validated through a case study of Miyazaki City, Japan, with sensitivity analysis.
